About The Library

A Hub for Academic Excellence

Aligned with the Davao Oriental State University's vision of excellence, innovation, and inclusion, the Cateel Campus Library serves as the primary academic support center for our students, faculty, and researchers.

We provide access to a vast collection of printed and electronic resources, comfortable study spaces, and dedicated research assistance to help our community thrive in their academic pursuits and become highly competent professionals.

  • Extensive physical & digital collections
  • Quiet study zones and collaborative spaces
  • Expert research and reference assistance
